When Ford brought back the Bronco in 2021, they made a design decision that every Jeep owner recognizes: put the roll cage where you can see it. Instead of burying the high-strength steel structure under headliner panels and interior trim, the full-size Bronco puts its roll cage front and center inside the cabin — powder-coated tubes running along the A-pillars, across the roofline, behind the rear seats, and at the sport bar position in the cargo area. It's a deliberate callback to the original Bronco's utilitarian DNA, and it creates a genuinely unique interior platform for off-road capability upgrades.

The 2021 Bronco launched with a standard roll cage, factory plastic grab handles, and a center console handhold. That's it. No overhead storage, no roll bar padding, no paracord grips, no rear recovery rack. The factory treated the cage as a safety feature and left everything else for the aftermarket. Five model years later — 2021 through 2026 — the aftermarket has responded with a full ecosystem of bolt-on cage accessories engineered around the Bronco's specific cage geometry. This guide covers the best of them, ranked for real trail and overland use.

Coverage here is for the full-size Ford Bronco 2021–2026, both 2-door and 4-door. The Bronco Sport is a different vehicle on an Escape-based platform with a hidden roll structure and different interior dimensions — nothing on this page applies to the Sport model.

Why the Ford Bronco Roll Cage Is the Best Aftermarket Platform in Its Class

The exposed roll cage design isn't unique to the Ford Bronco — Jeep Wranglers, Jeep Gladiators, and a handful of serious off-road rigs share this philosophy. But the Bronco's cage geometry is unusually well-suited to aftermarket accessories because Ford's engineers clearly thought about the cage as a mounting platform from the beginning. The cage tubes run at consistent, known diameters. There are existing threaded inserts and mounting points that bolt-on accessories can use without drilling. The sport bars at the rear of the cage are positioned at exactly the right height for grab handles and chase racks, and the cage bars above the rear seats are in exactly the right position for overhead storage panels.

This isn't an accident. Ford's engineering team benchmarked the Wrangler extensively before the 2021 Bronco launch, and the cage integration they built into the Bronco is better in several respects than what the Wrangler offers. The result is that the aftermarket for Bronco roll cage accessories developed quickly after 2021 and has matured into a full product ecosystem by 2026. Every category is well-covered with fitment-specific products — not universal-fit compromises.

The four main upgrade categories for the Bronco cage, in order of priority:

  • Roll bar padding — Wraps exposed cage tubing at passenger head height. The Bronco's A-pillar bars and interior cage tubes are exactly where head strikes happen in rollovers or hard off-road impacts. Fire-retardant foam padding is the lowest-cost, highest-safety-impact upgrade on this page. Do this before anything else.
  • Grab handles — Aftermarket paracord-wrapped handles mount to the roll cage bars and replace factory plastic loops with grips that function with gloves on in wet and muddy conditions. Essential for any Bronco with regular passengers on trail rides.
  • Overhead MOLLE storage panels — Bolt to the cage bars above the rear seats without drilling. Creates an overhead gear organization grid for first aid kits, communications equipment, flashlights, and trail tools that stays accessible and doesn't consume floor or cargo space.
  • Sport bar chase racks — Clamp to the rear sport bars of the cage and carry Hi-Lift jacks, full-size spares, shovels, and recovery boards at a stable, accessible position behind the rear cab. The critical upgrade for overland Broncos running remote terrain.
